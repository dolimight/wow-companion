How this is decided
  • Each check compares one named measure with one named group of players; nothing is combined into a single score
  • “You've timed it” means you finished this level or higher in time, in the selected dungeon or in at least half of this season's dungeons
  • “Within reach” means your item level and Mythic+ score are both at or above the median of players who timed this level
  • “Stretch” means one or both are under the median but still within the lower quarter; “Not yet” means at least one is below the lower quarter
  • A level more than one above your best timed key is “Not yet”, whatever the gear comparison says
  • With fewer than 30 timed runs in the comparison group, the level shows “Not enough data” instead of a guess
  • Gear, route, and group still decide a run; this shows how players like you have done, not a promise
