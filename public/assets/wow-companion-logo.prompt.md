## Original prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production app symbol for a Windows desktop application named WOW Companion, a personal World of Warcraft character, gear and wardrobe planner
Create one original, exceptionally clear logo mark: a compact angular W formed by two ascending ribbon strokes, embraced by one open C-shaped circular arc, subtly suggesting a compass and character progression. The symbol must read as one cohesive, balanced silhouette at 24 pixels. Restrained Apple-like precision, simple vector-friendly geometry, generous negative space, crisp edges, no fine details. Palette: luminous ice blue and clean white/silver, suitable for a pure black application interface and a light interface. A subtle blue tonal difference is fine; avoid metallic texture and glossy 3D effects. Centered standalone mark with about 12 percent clear padding on a square transparent canvas. True transparent alpha background. No background plate, no black square, no shadows, no glow, no slogan, no wordmark, no extra text, no Warcraft or Blizzard existing emblem, no presentation mockup, no multiple variants. Deliver a clean high-resolution PNG logo asset.

## Cleanup prompt

Edit this app logo into a clean production logo asset while retaining its recognizable W monogram, open C compass ring, four compass points, overall silhouette and blue/white identity. Remove EVERY stray speck, fleck, torn fragment, halo and rough artifact in the empty space around and inside the symbol. Make ALL contours perfectly crisp, smooth vector-like edges. Simplify the ribbon shading to solid restrained ice blue and white shapes with at most one secondary blue, no gradients, no glossy/metallic 3D texture, no shadows. Do not add text or change the overall composition. The empty background and openings must have genuine uniform fully transparent alpha, not a black color field. Output one centered logo on a square transparent canvas with clear padding, no mockup or extras.
